### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a **Full-Stack Software Developer** with a Management Information Systems (MIS) background, specializing in building scalable, maintainable enterprise web applications within the **C# / .NET** and **Angular** ecosystems. Combining strong architectural discipline with a background in **Unity game development** and hands-on **electronics/hardware curiosity**, I focus on turning complex business logic into clean, high-performance software products.
